| momentum DSL | -0.07 | -41.9% | -0.10 (0.12, -0.84, 0.73, -0.41) | pass | caught | 71% |
| reversal DSL | -0.06 | -13.7% | -0.33 (0.45, 0.44, -1.05, -1.15) | pass | caught | 64% |

Both DSL rules were weak: negative full-period Sharpe, deep drawdowns, and no consistent out-of-sample gain across folds. That agrees with the main P8 result that the price-only styles have no demonstrated edge after costs. The look-ahead checker passed the honest rules and caught the deliberate `.lead(1)` in both. Carry, value, macro and other styles need FRED data and were not run in ManifoldBT.

## Limitations

- Yahoo `=F` series are not back-adjusted; Yahoo volume is unreliable for several instruments, so impact and capacity are bracketed, not pinned.
- The H1 net Sharpe interval includes zero; only the H1-minus-v1 difference excludes it, and only at lag 2.
- The H1 rate was chosen by walk-forward and fixed afterwards; the grid-edge sensitivity is not in the prereg.
- D4 and D5 use 2001-2017 gross Sharpe, in-sample; a restricted style passing is descriptive.
- 2018-2026 is a seen period. Only the forward test can add unseen evidence.
- No taxes, financing or short-borrow costs, and no slippage beyond the stated cost and impact model.
